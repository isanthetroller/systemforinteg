    Password Reset Instructions

    Campus Gate & Security Administration

    To preserve campus audit integrity and security credentials, officer passwords cannot be reset automatically online.

    How to request credential recovery:
    • Contact the Campus IT Office or your Gate Security Supervisor.
    • Provide your official username and Badge Number (e.g. NCST-SEC-01).
    • A temporary access password will be granted for you to sign in and set a permanent credential.
