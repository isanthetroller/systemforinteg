How your pass works

  • Show the QR on the My Pass tab at the gate when entering and leaving campus.
  • The guard confirms that the driver is on your authorized drivers list.
  • Each warning is one strike. At 3 strikes your vehicle is banned until the Security Office resolves it.
  • Parking overnight or past curfew (22:00) adds a strike.
  • Changes to your vehicle or drivers are done at the Security Office. A new pass replaces the old one.
